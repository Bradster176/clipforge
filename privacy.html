Privacy Policy

Effective 8 October 2026

ClipForge is a personal desktop app, run by its owner on their own Windows PC, that turns livestream highlights into short vertical clips and posts them to the owner's own social media accounts (TikTok, YouTube, Instagram and Facebook). This policy explains what data it handles.

1Who runs ClipForge

ClipForge is operated by Brad, an individual creator in the United Kingdom. It is not offered to the public, and the only accounts connected to it are the operator's own.

2Data from connected platforms

When an account is connected through TikTok Login Kit (or Google or Meta sign-in), ClipForge receives:

  • basic profile info: account ID, display name and avatar, used only to show which account is connected;
  • access and refresh tokens, used only to upload clips the operator has chosen to post (approved by hand or by posting rules the operator sets).

ClipForge does not read followers, messages, comments, analytics or any other account data.

3Where data is stored

Everything stays on the operator's PC. Tokens are stored in Windows Credential Manager and are never written to logs. ClipForge has no servers or cloud database and does not send this data to anyone apart from the platform it is posting to.

4Content that gets posted

ClipForge posts only the video, title, caption and hashtags of a clip the operator has chosen to post, to the operator's own account. Videos are clips of public livestreams.

5Sharing and selling

No data is sold, rented or shared with advertisers or other third parties. There is no tracking and there are no cookies.

6Keeping and deleting data

Tokens are kept until the account is disconnected in ClipForge, which deletes them straight away. You can also revoke access at any time in TikTok under Settings → Security and permissions → Apps and services, and in your Google or Meta account settings.

7Children

ClipForge is not directed at children and does not knowingly process children's data.

8Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published at this address with a new effective date.
